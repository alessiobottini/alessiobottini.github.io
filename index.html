I am a Chapman Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at Imperial College London. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in Bonn, working with Daniel Huybrechts. I received my Ph.D. in November 2023 from Université Paris-Saclay and Tor Vergata University under the supervision of Emanuele Macrì and Antonio Rapagnetta. Here is my CV.

I work in algebraic geometry, primarily on hyper-Kähler varieties and related topics. In particular, I study sheaves on hyper-Kähler varieties and their moduli spaces, with the aim of developing a higher-dimensional analogue of the theory for K3 surfaces. More recently, I have also become interested in Brauer groups and the period–index conjecture.
